FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Does it work with Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer or Anghami?

No, and no tool does. Those services stream DRM-protected audio with no downloadable file. Paste the song name instead: Naghma searches YouTube Music, downloads the matching audio and tags it for you.

Why does the first launch download ~170 MB?

Naghma is a 63 MB app; the engine (yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno) is fetched once into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Naghma\bin and reused. If you already have FFmpeg on your PATH, that download is skipped.

Does the Pro key need internet?

No. It is an ECDSA signature checked inside the app. No activation server, no phone-home, no expiry. Reinstall Windows and the same key still works.

Can I use one key on several computers?

Yes — the key is tied to your email, not to a machine. Keep it reasonable: it is meant for the computers you personally use.

What happens when YouTube changes something?

The engine updates itself. Update engine pulls the latest yt-dlp in seconds; you rarely need a new app version.

Is there a Mac or Linux build?

Not yet. The engine is cross-platform, the interface is WinForms, so a port means a rewrite of the UI.

If it does not work on your machine, ask within 14 days and you get your money back — no interrogation.
